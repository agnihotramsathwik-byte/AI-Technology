NexaAI — AI Technology Website

Files:
- index.html : webpage structure and content
- style.css  : visual design, layout, animations, and responsive styling
- script.js  : mobile navigation, scroll reveal, and demo contact form

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. For best results, use an internet connection so the Google Fonts can load.

Customization:
- Replace NexaAI branding, product copy, and team names with your own.
- Replace hello@nexaai.example with your real contact email.
- The contact form currently opens the visitor's email client. Connect it to a backend/API to collect messages directly.
